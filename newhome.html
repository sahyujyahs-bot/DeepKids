The Mantle35–2,890 km

Six Rules We Don't Break

Every DeepKids box has to clear all six before it ships. This is the part that takes the months.

01

Scientifically Accurate

Checked, argued over, kept current — including what's still being debated. We'd rather say "nobody fully knows yet" than hand over a tidy lie.

02

One Topic, All The Way Down

Not trivia sprayed across a hundred subjects. One concept from many angles, deep enough that it connects to something real.

03

Experienced, Not Explained

Activities built so a child feels the concept first — balancing, tossing, blowing, jumping. A force you've fought with your body needs no mugging up.

04

Appropriately Challenging

Our games are not the easiest, by design. Hand a child a genuine first-principles question and they'll turn it over in ways most adults have forgotten how to.

05

Made, Not Manufactured

100% original hand-drawn artwork, premium materials, small batches. Good design and real quality, because a thing worth keeping gets played again.

06

Modular Gameplay

Solo, family or group, with multiple modes, built-in levels and extension packs — so the box keeps earning its place on the shelf.
