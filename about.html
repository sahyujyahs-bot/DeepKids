About DeepKids

The studio behind EscapeGravity

DeepKids started from a simple belief: kids are deep. Far deeper than we usually give them credit for. A child's mind isn't held back by years of "that's just how it is" — it can sit with an abstract idea, turn it over, and ask the kind of first-principles questions a set-in-its-ways adult brain has long stopped asking.

I built EscapeGravity to give that kind of mind something worthy to chew on.

What we stand for

  • Science, not simplification. Every concept on the board is rooted in real, accurate science — including the parts where scientists themselves disagree. We'd rather hand a kid a genuine, contested idea than a tidy lie.
  • First principles over memorised answers. The game is built to be reasoned through, not looked up. We want kids walking away with a way of thinking, not just a fact.
  • Free thinking, not a worksheet. No grading, no "correct" box to tick. Just a game world strange enough to make you want to figure out how it works.

Why a board game

Physics, gravity, force — these are things kids usually only read about. We wanted to make them physical: something you can hold, push, fail at, and try again, with friends, off a screen. EscapeGravity is the first attempt at that. It won't be the last.
