Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Availability: EscapeGravity, SCI. Trading Cards and The Story Of Evolution are printed and in stock. Orders are dispatched from our Bengaluru workshop and this policy applies to every one of them.

1. Cancellation Before Shipment

You may cancel your pre-order at any time before your order has been shipped and receive a full refund. To cancel, contact us via:

  • Email: sahyujyahs@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: we will message you from the number we contact you on

Cancellation requests are processed within 2 business days. Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after confirmation.

2. Refund After Delivery

If you receive your order and it is:

  • Damaged or defective — we will replace or refund at no extra cost. Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage.
  • Incorrect item received — we will ship the correct item or issue a full refund.
  • Missing components — we will ship the missing parts or offer a replacement/refund.

3. Change of Mind Returns

We accept returns for change of mind within 7 days of delivery, provided:

  • The product is unused and in its original packaging
  • All components are intact
  • Return shipping is arranged by the customer at their cost

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, a refund (minus original shipping cost) will be processed within 5–7 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Products that have been used, opened, or are no longer in original condition
  • Returns initiated more than 7 days after delivery (except for defects)
  • Orders where incorrect address was provided by the customer leading to non-delivery

5. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved:

  • UPI / Net Banking: 3–5 business days
  • Credit / Debit Card: 5–7 business days
  • Wallets: 1–3 business days

Refunds are processed back to your original UPI ID / bank account.

6. Shipping for Pre-Orders

Shipping is included in the pre-order price. You will be notified via WhatsApp with a tracking link once your order is dispatched.

7. Contact Us

For any refund, cancellation, or order queries:

  • Email: sahyujyahs@gmail.com
  • Website: deepkids.in

We aim to respond to all queries within 1 business day.
