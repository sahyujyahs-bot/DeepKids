Privacy

DeepKids Gurukul, THE homeschooling app · app.deepkids.in · last updated 27 October 2026

DeepKids Gurukul helps parents plan and teach their children at home. This page says what we keep, why, and what you can do about it.

What we keep

  • Your sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them only to sign you in and to show your name in the app.
  • Your family's plan: what you tell the app, such as your child's first name, age, class and board, your timetable, lessons done, quiz answers and notes.
  • Things you add: photos, worksheets and files you upload, such as your child's work or your bookshelf.

How we use it

  • To plan your days, track progress and show your child's work back to you.
  • For DeepKids Plus features (IAm, sorting notes and photos, entrance tests): the text or photo you send is passed to Anthropic's Claude AI to write the answer. It is not used to train their models.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Your account and files are stored with Supabase, our database provider. They are private to your account: other families cannot see them.

Children

The app is used by parents. Children may use it with a parent, under the parent's account. We only keep the child details the parent chooses to add.

Your choices

  • You can save a full copy of your data from the app at any time (Settings, "Save a copy").
  • To delete your account and everything in it, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.
