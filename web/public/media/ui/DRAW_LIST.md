# Drawings for the app (founder, Procreate)

Export each as a **PNG with a transparent background**, about **1500 px** on the long side. Send them here in the chat, or commit them to `app/content/media/ui/drawn/`. Keep each one simple: one or two things, lots of empty space.

## Characters (most important)
1. **Girl, standing**, front view, arms relaxed.
2. **Boy, standing**, front view.
3. **Parent**, standing.
4. One of the children in a few poses, if you can: **pointing up**, **sitting cross-legged**, **reading**, **jumping with joy**.

## Motifs (small, used everywhere)
5. An **eye** (your third-eye style)
6. A **spiral**
7. A **star**, and a **crescent moon**
8. A **sun**
9. A **leaf or seedling**

## Optional, if you have time
10. A **night sky band** (wide, about 3:1), for the top of Today and sign-in
11. A **path or doorway**, for the DeepKids track
12. An **open book**, for the Board track

I'll build every scene from these pieces: the subject pictures, the track pictures, the rest-day and all-done pictures, the sign-in slides and the empty screens. Everything then carries your line.
