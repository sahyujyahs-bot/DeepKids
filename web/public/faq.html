Common questions

DeepKids Gurukul · a homeschooling app for Indian families · app.deepkids.in

What is DeepKids Gurukul?

DeepKids Gurukul is a homeschooling app for Indian families with children aged 6 to 10. It plans each day, guides the parent through every lesson and activity, and keeps track of what the child has learned. It runs in a phone or computer browser at app.deepkids.in.

Which ages and classes is it for?

Children aged 6 to 10, which is about Class 1 to Class 5.

Which boards and syllabuses does it follow?

Families can follow NCERT (CBSE), Cambridge Primary or NIOS, chapter by chapter. State boards that follow the NCERT pattern can use the NCERT track. Waldorf-style teaching is supported as a way of teaching, not as a board. There is also the DeepKids track, which is led by topics instead of a textbook.

Do I need to be a teacher to use it?

No. Each lesson tells the parent what to have ready, what to do and what to ask. No teaching experience is needed.

Do I need to buy textbooks?

Not on the DeepKids track, which has its own lessons, experiments, games and worksheets. On a board track the plan follows the book the family chooses.

Which subjects does it cover?

Maths, English, Hindi and Science, plus the Waldorf-style subjects for families who choose that way of teaching. DeepKids' own topic units are most complete in Science, and Maths is growing. For English, Hindi and Maths the DeepKids track uses the NCERT lessons for now.

Can I try it without signing in?

Yes. Choose Continue without signing in, tell the app about your child, and Today is filled in straight away. Without signing in, the plan is kept on that phone only. Signing in with Google later keeps the plan safe and brings it along.

Does it use AI?

Every feature works without AI, for every family. AI help is an optional extra for families on DeepKids Plus, for things like reading a photo of a worksheet, checking answers, chat and writing new material.

What does a day look like?

Today shows what to do and when: lessons, a warm-up, projects, classes and outings, shaped around the family's timetable and pace. Progress shows hours spent, what was done each day, and how the child compares with a school year.

Is my family's information private?

The app keeps only what it needs to run the family's plan. Read the details on the privacy page at app.deepkids.in/privacy.html.

Where does it work?

In any modern phone or computer browser, and it can be added to the phone's home screen like an app.
