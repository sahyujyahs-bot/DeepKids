DeepKids Gurukul

A homeschooling app for Indian families · app.deepkids.in

DeepKids Gurukul is a homeschooling app for Indian families with children aged 6 to 10. It plans each day, guides the parent through every lesson, and keeps track of what the child has learned. It is made by DeepKids, whose shop is at deepkids.in.

Open the app

What it does

  • A plan for every day. Today shows what to do and when: lessons, a warm-up, projects, classes and outings. It moves with the family's timetable and pace.
  • A guide for the parent. Each lesson says what to have ready, what to do, and what to ask. No teaching experience needed.
  • Progress you can see. Hours spent, what was done each day, and how the child compares with a school year.
  • Works without AI. Every feature works for every family. AI help is an extra for families on DeepKids Plus.

Two ways to learn

Board track. Follows the family's chosen book, chapter by chapter: NCERT, Cambridge Primary or NIOS. Each chapter comes with extra activities, questions and games.

DeepKids track. The DeepKids own syllabus, led by topics instead of a textbook. It covers what every board teaches in that class, and goes deeper into each idea with full lessons, experiments, games and worksheets. Waldorf-style teaching is also supported as a way of teaching, not a board.

Who it is for

Parents homeschooling or after-schooling children aged 6 to 10 (about Class 1 to Class 5) in India, in any city or town.

Privacy

Families sign in with Google, and the app keeps only what it needs to run their plan. Read the details on the privacy page.
