# Science: concepts the boards teach that our plan does not yet cover

Built 26 Nov 2026 by comparing 715 board concepts (ICSE, Cambridge, NCERT, NIOS; Classes 1-5) with our 722 planned science units. Judged by Sonnet, one pass per class; the lists below are what it flagged. Spot-check of 12 random 'covered' answers: 7 held clearly, 4 were craft or drawing activities (not science concepts, fine to ignore), 1 was a weak match (reducing non-degradable garbage), so treat 'covered' as mostly right, not perfect.

**Missing** = no planned unit teaches it. **Partial** = a unit touches it but misses part. **Early/Late** = only taught at a level well away from the board's class. Social-studies items (NIOS/NCERT) are listed separately.

**Totals (grouped entries):** missing 32 · partial 61 · early/late 24 · social 20
