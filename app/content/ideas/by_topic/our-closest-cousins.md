## SCI Cards: scientists for this topic (founder's card game; strongest underdog stories first)
- **Jane Goodall** (1934-04-03–Living, English; World Changer, Heretic, Foundational): Proved that chimpanzees make and use tools — overturning the definition of humans as the only tool-using species. Discovered that chimps hunt, eat meat, have complex social hierarchies, wage wars, show empathy, and mourn their dead. Her 65+ years of continuous research at Gombe is the longest-running animal study in history. *Whisper:* The line between humans and animals is thinner than we thought. Chimpanzees make tools, grieve, wage war, and show compassion. We are not as special as we believed — and that's not diminishing, it's connecting. *Story:* Sent to Tanzania in 1960 by Louis Leakey with no degree and no formal training — just a notebook and binoculars. Within months she observed chimps making and using tools (stripping leaves from twigs to fish for termites). When she told Leakey, he said: 'Now we must redefine tool, redefine man, or accept chimpanzees as human.' The scientific establishment attacked her for naming her subjects instead of numbering them and for attributing emotions to animals. She was right, they were wrong. Over 65 years, her Gombe research revealed a mirror — showing us that much of what we call 'human' exists in our closest relatives.
