- **Sandra Faber** (1944-12-28–Living, American; World Changer, Foundational, Applied): Co-discovered the 'Great Attractor' — a gravitational anomaly pulling our galaxy and thousands of others toward it at 600 km/s. Faber-Jackson relation (galaxy luminosity correlates with star velocity — a tool for measuring cosmic distances). Diagnosed the Hubble Space Telescope's flawed mirror and led the team that designed the corrective optics (COSTAR). *Whisper:* Our entire galaxy — 200 billion stars — is being pulled through space at 2 million km/h toward something so massive we can't even see it yet. Gravity operates at scales that dwarf imagination. *Story:* Faber's career spans some of the most dramatic discoveries in cosmology. She co-discovered that the Milky Way and nearby galaxies are all flowing toward a mysterious gravitational concentration — the Great Attractor — at 600 km/s. When the Hubble Space Telescope launched with a flawed mirror in 1990 (producing blurry images), Faber diagnosed the exact nature of the defect and led the team that designed corrective optics. The fix worked perfectly — every stunning Hubble image since 1993 exists because Faber's team saved the telescope.
