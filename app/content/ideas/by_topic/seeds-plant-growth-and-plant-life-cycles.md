## SCI Cards: scientists for this topic (founder's card game; strongest underdog stories first)
- **Nikolai Vavilov** (25 Nov 1887–1943-01-26, Russian / Soviet; Heretic, World Changer, Foundational, Underdog, Forbidden Knowledge): Identified the centers of origin of cultivated plants (where crops were first domesticated). Built the world's largest seed bank (250,000+ samples). Developed the theory of crop diversity as protection against famine. Proposed the law of homologous series in variation — predicting traits in related species. *Whisper:* The genetic diversity of seeds IS the insurance policy for human civilization. Lose it, and one disease could wipe out a crop that feeds billions. Diversity is not a luxury — it is survival. *Story:* Vavilov traveled five continents collecting seeds to protect humanity against famine through crop diversity. His rival, Trofim Lysenko — a politically connected pseudoscientist who rejected genetics — convinced Stalin that Vavilov's Mendelian genetics was 'bourgeois pseudoscience.' Vavilov was arrested, tortured, and sentenced to death (later commuted to 20 years). He died of starvation in a Soviet prison in 1943 — the man who devoted his life to ending famine, starved to death. During the Siege of Leningrad, his colleagues CHOSE to starve to death rather than eat the seed bank he built. Twelve scientists died protecting those seeds.
