# Unit idea: white pomegranate, albinism and pale living things

Idea from the founder (25 Nov 2026), seen after finding a white pomegranate. To be written later; remind her.

- Working title: "White on purpose? Pale plants, fruits and animals". Science, next to sci.genetics.dna-and-rna (the cause is a gene that doesn't work). Level 3 or Level 2: ask her.
- Core truth: "white" has several causes.
  - White pomegranate, white strawberry, white jamun: varieties with little red pigment (anthocyanin). Healthy, normal plants. Not albino.
  - Albino plant: no chlorophyll. Seedling is white, cannot make food, dies when the seed's store runs out.
  - Albino animal: no melanin; pink or red eyes. Leucistic animal: less pigment, not none; dark eyes (a different condition). Examples to check before use: white tigers, white peacocks, white crows.
- Open question: why do some living things lose a colour and keep living?
