# History Board Game / Mhajanapadas empires & capitals (copied from Google Sheets, 2026-11-27)
R1: # | Janapada | Capital (ancient) | Core region (today) | Notes
R2: 1 | Aṅga | Campā | East Bihar–West Bengal (Bhagalpur area) | Early rival/target of Magadha.
R3: 2 | Magadha | Rājagṛha → Pāṭaliputra | South–central Bihar (Patna, Rajgir) | The ultimate unifier (Nandas → Mauryas).
R4: 3 | Kāśi | Vārāṇasī | Eastern U.P. | Fought seesaw wars with Kośala/Magadha.
R5: 4 | Kośala | Śrāvastī (also Sāketa/Ayodhyā) | Oudh region, U.P. | Powerful monarchs (Prasenajit); Buddha’s era.
R6: 5 | Vajji (Vṛji) Confederacy | Vaiśālī | North Bihar (Mithila/Tirhut) | Gaṇa-sangha (republican league: Licchavis, Videhas, etc.).
R7: 6 | Malla | Kuśinārā & Pāvā | North Bihar–East U.P. | Republican clans; Buddha’s parinirvāṇa at Kuśinārā.
R8: 7 | Cedi (Cedi/Cheti) | Suktimatī (site debated) | Bundelkhand–Narmada belt (MP/UP) | Old lineage; location uncertain in archaeology.
R9: 8 | Vatsa | Kauśāmbī | Allahabad/Prayagraj–Kaushambi (U.P.) | Prosperous trading monarchy (Udayana).
R10: 9 | Kuru | Indraprastha (& Iśukāra in some lists) | Delhi–Haryana | Later a gaṇa element; Vedic prestige zone.
R11: 10 | Pañcāla | Ahicchatra (North), Kampilya (South) | Rohilkhand–Doab (U.P.) | Mixed monarchical/republican phases.
R12: 11 | Matsya | Vīrāṭanagara (modern Bairāt) | NE Rajasthan–Alwar/Jaipur | Smaller but strategic, near Aravallis.
R13: 12 | Sūrasena | Mathurā | Western U.P. | On Yamunā trade; later Krishna-cult heartland.
R14: 13 | Avanti | Ujjayinī & Mahishmatī | Western & southern MP | Western powerhouse; rival of Magadha.
R15: 14 | Gāndhāra | Takṣaśilā | NW Pakistan–Afghanistan | University/cosmopolitan hub; Achaemenid contact.
R16: 15 | Kamboja | Rājapura/Kapiśa (disputed) | Kabul–Kunar–Swat belt | Trans-Himalayan horse culture; often republican.
R17: 16 | Assaka (Aśmaka) | Potali (Paudanya) | Godāvarī valley (N Maharashtra/Telangana) | The outlier to the south among the 16.
