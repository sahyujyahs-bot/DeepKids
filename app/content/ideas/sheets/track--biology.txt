R13: Homeostasis | Your body’s balance-keeper. It keeps temperature, water, and energy just right so you can play, think, and sleep. | (1) Feedback loops (when too hot → cool down). (2) Set points (like a comfy room temp). (3) Sensors–controllers–effectors (skin → brain → sweat glands). | Balance is not stillness; it’s gentle adjusting like a surfer staying on a wave. We learn to notice signals and respond kindly to the body. | Sweat Lab: Hand in warm water vs. fan; track skin temp with touch/ice cube timing. [P+C] (bowl, fan, ice). Salty Sip: Taste tiny pinches of salt in water to feel osmosis thirst signals (safe amounts). [P+C] Sugar Steady: Eat a small snack (fruit/crackers), play for 10 min, notice energy change; chart feelings. [Solo] | Thermostat Hunt: Find household “balancers” (fridge, AC, dimmer). Label with sticky notes. [P+C] Clothing Dial: Try layers, jump 1 min, remove/add and notice comfort. [Solo] Water Whisperer: Place 2 cups; drink small sips every 15 mins; note bathroom trips. [Solo] | Shade Chaser: Walk sun→shade; feel temp change; measure by time-to-sweaty. [Friends] Leaf Guard: Mist one plant, not the other; check wilting. [P+C] Puddle Patrol: Observe puddle shrinking; wind vs sun. [Solo] | Thermo Groove: Move slow when “snowflake”, fast when “sunbeam”, freeze when “just right”. [Friends] Fan-and-Flame: Partner fans with paper plate when music says “hot”. [P+C] Pulse Party: Dance 30s, check pulse, breathe down. [Solo] | Body Council: Kid plays “Brain Mayor”, parent as “Sweat Team”, plushies are “Sensors”; improv scenes (too hot kitchen!). [P+C] Crisis Call: A cookie monster wants more sugar; council decides balance plan. [Friends] Thirsty Planet: River town saves water. [Zoom] | Balance Bar Graph: Draw you in three weathers; color zones: hot/cold/just right. [Solo] Feedback Arrows: Blue arrows for cool-down, red for warm-up. [P+C] Set-Point Sun: Paint a thermostat with a smile when comfy. [Solo] | Tempo Thermostat: Clap fast (hot), slow (cold), medium (just right); switch on cue. [Friends] Rainstick Reset: Shake → breathe until beads stop = calm return. [Solo] Drum & Hum: Drum 10 beats, hum to settle. [P+C] | Cooling Cloud: Inhale 4, “blow soup” exhale 6; hand on belly. [Solo] Ocean Ears: Cup ears, listen to breath like waves. [Solo] Starfish Hand: Trace inhale/exhale along fingers. [P+C] | Set-Point Relay: Run to “Hot/Cold/Just Right” cones on parent cues. [Friends] Sweater Sprint: Wear sweater, run 30s, remove, compare feel. [P+C] Water Token Tag: Earn “sips” tokens after laps. [Friends] | Card Dial: Card game where red raises temp, blue cools; play to keep meter at 5. [Friends] Balance Bingo: Squares: drink water, rest, stretch, snack. [Solo] Feedback Flip: Flip a coin: heads = too hot, tails = too cold; act solution. [P+C] | Short reads: “Your Amazing Body Thermostat” (1‑page zine). Plant Water Watch mini article. Why We Sweat fact cards. | Notebook: “Today my body said… I listened by…”. Poem: “I am a thermostat.” Survey: Ask 3 family members their comfy temp. | Kitchen Chemistry Lite: Warm/room/cool water taste tests. Sock Thermometer: Wet vs dry sock on wrists (supervised). Ice Tower: Melt race under fan vs towel. | Mindful Snack: Apple slices + peanut butter → energy check-in. Shadow Breaks: Schedule 3 five‑minute calm resets. Bath Thermometer: Safe warm water test with elbow. | Cardboard Control Panel: Knobs for “water, warmth, calm, energy”. [P+C] Paper Fan: Decorate cool-down fans. Sweat Gland Puppets: Paper strip “sprayers”. | “Balance Me” Poster Pack: Printable meters, habit stickers, daily check chart. Feedback Loop Flipbook: Hot→sweat→cool sequence. Family Thermostat Pact: Rules everyone co-writes for summer/winter comfort. | Explainer: Homeostasis = body balance via feedback loops. Sensors (skin, brain) notice changes; effectors (sweat, shiver) adjust. Food & water fuel steady energy; rest returns to baseline. | Prompts: “What is your body asking for?” “Is this too hot, too cold, or just right?” “What’s one tiny dial you can turn now?” | Roles: Parent = Narrator (sets scenes), Safety Monitor (temp/food allergies), Co‑player (joins relays), Timekeeper (check‑ins every 10–15 min). Prep bowls/ice/fan, shade plan, water bottles. | Scripts: “Sensor says: cheeks warm. Brain Mayor calls Sweat Team!” “Our goal is ‘just right,’ not ‘perfect.’ What’s your next tiny dial?” “Try water first; then re‑check.” | Reflect: “When did a small change help a lot?” “Which dial (water, clothing, rest) you used most today?” “What body message do you ignore sometimes?”
R14: Nervous & endocrine integration | Two messenger systems team up. Nerves send fast zaps; hormones send slow waves through blood. Together they plan, react, and keep balance. | (1) Speed vs duration. (2) Network (nerves) vs broadcast (hormones). (3) Integration: brain checks hormones; glands respond to brain. | Wisdom can be quick or slow. We practice matching the message to the moment—sprint or stroll, whisper or town‑announcement. | Reaction Ruler: Drop a ruler; measure catch distance (nerves). [Solo] Paper “Hormone” Diffusion: Food dye in water spreading (slow signal). [P+C] Reflex Tap: Knee reflex (gentle, supervised) vs deliberate kick (choice). [P+C] | Signal Stations: Place bells in rooms; one bell = nerve (instant), many bells = hormone (echo). [P+C] Kitchen Timer Test: 10‑sec rapid math vs 10‑min puzzle; which system fits? [Solo] Scent Messenger: Vanilla in one room diffuses; note timing. [P+C] | Bird Flock: Watch birds/squirrels; fast flinch vs calm forage. [Solo] Flower Clock: Note when blooms open/close (slow rhythms). [P+C] Trail Tasks: Sprint to a tree on clap (fast); wait 2 mins before snack (slow). [Friends] | Zap & Wave: Sharp moves on drum hits (nerve), flowing moves on sustained notes (hormone). [Friends] Stop‑Start Tai‑chi: Freeze–flow cycles. [P+C] Metronome Moods: Fast beat = alert dance; slow beat = calm sway. [Solo] | Brain HQ: Kid as “Commander Neuron,” parent as “Gland Council”; improv emergencies (bee! test! bedtime!). [P+C] Post‑Office Play: Letters (hormones) vs phone calls (nerves). [Friends] Weather Newsroom: Breaking news vs weekly forecast. [Zoom] | Neural Pathways Map: Draw roads from sense organs to brain; traffic lights = decisions. [Solo] Gland Garden: Sketch pituitary “sprinkler,” thyroid “sun,” pancreas “candy shop.” [P+C] Signal Comics: 4‑panel: problem → nerve ping → hormone help → balance. [Solo] | Call & Echo: Clap (call) + ooooh (echo). [Friends] Instrument Roles: Snare = nerves; cello = hormones. [P+C] Pulse to Piano: Play fast then slow to match heartbeat. [Solo] | Bee Breath (bhramari): hum to calm slow system. Box Breath 4‑4‑4‑4 for steady signals. Rocket Breath: 3 quick sniffs (alert), long exhale (settle). | Neuron Tag: Tag = nerve zaps; safe zones = glands releasing “shields”. [Friends] Balance Course: Quick hop then long hold pose. [P+C] Message Relay: Whisper chain vs written note passed around. [Zoom/Friends] | Card Draft: “Use Nerve or Hormone?” scenario cards. [Friends] Reflex Catch: Toss‑and‑clap patterns. [Friends] Endo‑Bingo: Squares: “hydrated”, “slept 9h”, “ate breakfast”, etc. [Solo] | Short reads: “Who’s in the Gland Council?” Mini‑bio of neurons. Daily Rhythms one‑pager. | Notebook: “A time I needed fast help…” Comic: “The Day Thyroid Slept In.” List: 5 slow habits that help me think fast. | Gelatin Diffusion: Dye in gelatin vs water (slower!) Lemon Alert: Smell lemon → salivate (integration). Sound & Startle: Pop a paper bag (warn first), track pulse jump + recovery. | Light Hygiene: Dim lights 1 hr pre‑bed for melatonin. Snack Plan: Protein + fruit for steady attention. Alarm Audit: Gentle sunrise alarm instead of loud buzzer. | Pipe‑Cleaner Neuron: Dendrites/axon model. Cardboard Endocrine Map: Flaps for glands. Signal Spinner: A spinner that lands on “fast/slow/both” to decide responses. | “Body Signals Playbook” Zine: Kid‑made guide of fast vs slow choices. Scenario Deck: Printable 30‑card pack with everyday dilemmas. Group Play Kit: Zoom slides + cue cards for stop/flow games. | Explainer: Nerves = quick electric messages along wires (neurons). Endocrine = chemical messages (hormones) in blood, slower but longer‑lasting. Brain and glands talk both ways to match needs. | Prompts: “Is this a sprint or marathon moment?” “What would the Gland Council suggest?” “How will today’s sleep help tomorrow’s thinking?” | Roles: Parent = Game Master (sets scenarios), Safety (noise/startle), Coach (sleep/light/food routines), Modeler (self‑talk: “I’ll choose a slow solution”). | Scripts: “Call the nerves for now, and book the hormones for later.” “We’ll make a fast choice, then a slow check.” “Let’s ask Pancreas: need steady fuel?” | Reflect: “Which system did you use more today?” “Where did slow signals save the day?” “What routine helps your brain best?”
