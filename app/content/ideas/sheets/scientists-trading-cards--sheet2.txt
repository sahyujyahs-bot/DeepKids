Over time, dark matter became a central tenet of cosmology. Rubin herself was a champion for women in science as well. Despite her monumental find, she never won a Nobel Prize, a point often lamented in retrospect. | Rubin was long underappreciated. In an era when women in astronomy faced steep barriers, her work was not taken seriously at first. Eventually, she won many honors (except the Nobel, which many feel she deserved). The Nobel committee’s omission is frequently called “an egregious snub”. Thus, Vera Rubin’s legacy is twofold: scientifically, she revealed one of the universe’s great secrets, and socially, she exemplified how recognition can lag behind achievement. Today, dark matter is a pillar of physics, and Rubin – once an underdog – is now rightly regarded as a key figure who reshaped gravity research.
