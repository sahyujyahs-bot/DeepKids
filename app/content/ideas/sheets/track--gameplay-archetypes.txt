# DeepKids HomeSchooling Track / GamePlay Archetypes (copied from Google Sheets, 2026-11-27)
R1: 🧰 Gameplay Archetypes to Rotate
R3: Here’s a starter set — so each topic can land in a unique way:
R4: Cycle / Relay Games
R5: For concepts that are about flow, transformation, continuity.
R6: Eg: Energy, Water Cycle, Food Chain.
R7: Mystery / Detective Play
R8: For concepts about hidden layers, cause-effect, investigation.
R9: Eg: Causality/Karma, Atoms/Particles, Consciousness.
R10: Contradiction / Paradox Games
R11: For concepts that hold two opposite truths at once.
R12: Eg: Reality (real/not real), Quantum Physics (particle/wave), Zero & Infinity.
R13: Arena / Debate Play
R14: For concepts about clashing forces, multiple viewpoints.
R15: Eg: Fringe Science, Time Travel ethics, Consciousness as universal vs. human-only.
R16: Performance / Embodiment Games
R17: For concepts that need to be felt in the body.
R18: Eg: Gravity (fall, jump, balance), Sound (vibrate, sing, echo).
R19: Puzzle / Construction Games
R20: For concepts about structure, order, emergence.
R21: Eg: Symmetry, Crystals, DNA, Math patterns.
R22: Exploration / Mapping Games
R23: For concepts about scale, place, orientation.
R24: Eg: Universe history, Maps of space, Wormholes.
R25: Economy / Resource Games
R26: For concepts about limits, conservation, trade-offs.
R27: Eg: Entropy, Energy efficiency, Zero-sum.
R28: Story / Narrative Chain
R29: For concepts that unfold over time and sequence.
R30: Eg: Evolution story, History of Science, Causality strings.
R31: Hidden Role / Secret Information Games
R32: For concepts where observation changes outcome.
R33: Eg: Observer Effect, Entanglement, Illusion/Mask.
