# Checking the notebook before it reaches children

The ideas bank is the founder's raw notes. Parts were drafted quickly or with AI help, so writers must check every fact. Keep what is true, label what is debated, and drop what is wrong. These are the known trouble spots.

## Wrong or invented: do not teach as fact
- **Aryabhata's dates.** The gravity scientists table says "4th–2nd century BCE". Aryabhata lived 476–550 CE (the Āryabhaṭīya dates to 499 CE).
- **"Bose and the Whisper of the Moonlight"** (Delivery sheet). This is an imagined scene, not a recorded experiment. It can be used only if it is plainly labelled as a made-up story.
- **"Tesla and the Artificial Aurora" / "the Night the Earth Sang".** The Colorado Springs work of 1899 is real (the giant coil, the artificial lightning, the town's power failure). A man-made aurora and the storm "answering" him are not documented. Tesla did not "discover alternating current". He invented the AC induction motor and polyphase system, which won the "current war" against Edison's DC.
- **Entanglement as communication.** Entangled particles stay correlated, but no message or "telepathy" passes between them. They cannot be used to send information.
- **"ISS surfing gravity waves".** As a metaphor for orbit, use "surfing a slope of gravity". *Gravitational waves* are something else: ripples in spacetime, detected by LIGO in 2015.
- **"Bacteria solve mazes".** The famous maze-solver is a slime mould (*Physarum polycephalum*, Nakagaki 2000), which is not a bacterium. Bacteria do sense and steer (chemotaxis).
- **"Humans have the most intrusive Default Mode Network / the most dopamine-addicted brain of all animals".** No evidence supports either claim. Other mammals also have a default-mode-like network.

## Debated: teach as an open question, with both sides
- **Do plants feel?** Bose really did measure electrical responses in plants, and showed that chloroform and other anaesthetics dampen them. A 2017 study (Yokawa et al.) found anaesthetics also stop the Venus flytrap and touch-me-not moving. Whether plants *feel* pain or emotions is unknown. The T/F card "Plants feel pain (T)" should become "Scientists disagree".
- **Do trees talk to each other?** Fungal networks link roots and pass some carbon and signals. How much trees "share" or "talk" is disputed (Karst et al., 2023, *Nature Ecology & Evolution*). The card "Trees don't talk (F)" should be framed as a debate.
- **Thinking without a brain** (basal cognition, "thought predates neurons"). Single cells sense, decide and remember simple patterns (Michael Levin, Pamela Lyon and others). Whether to call that "thinking" is a live debate. Present it as a big question, which is exactly the DeepKids way.
- **Ancient Indian ideas of gravity.** Kaṇāda's *gurutva* (heaviness) and Brahmagupta's statement that the Earth attracts bodies are real. Reading a modern law of gravitation into Rig Veda verses is a modern interpretation, so mark it as debated. Credit old thinkers fully, but do not inflate them.
- **"Credit Thief" tags** (for example on Newton). The disputes with Hooke, Flamsteed and Leibniz are real. Present them as disputes with evidence on each side, and side with the underdog only where the record supports it.
- **More microbes than human cells.** The old 10:1 figure is outdated. The best estimate (Sender, Fuchs & Milo, 2016) is about 1.3 bacteria per human cell: roughly equal.
- **Evolution dates** (RNA ~4.2 billion years ago, first cells ~3.9, Asgard archaea ~3.5). These are estimates with wide error bars. Say "about" and "scientists think".

## Personal philosophy: a lens, not a lesson
- **The founder's view of consciousness** is DeepKids' root (see `../PHILOSOPHY.md`). We say it openly as what DeepKids believes and many sages taught, never as what science has found. It covers consciousness as the field, "order pockets" in chaos and the three realms, and guides the questions DeepKids asks. Offer it as one way of seeing, next to others. Never state it as science. For example, ask: "Could a particle have something like a self? Most physicists would say no; some philosophers (panpsychists) wonder." Never say: "Particles have a sense of self."
- **Genetics & gender notes.** The basics are fine: the X chromosome carries many more genes than the Y, and SRY on the Y starts testis development. The claims chaining androgen receptors to aggression, rank and risk are contested and oversimplified. Do not put them in child content. Note also that most of the second X in XX cells is switched off (X-inactivation), so it is only a partial "backup copy".

## Fine as written (spot-checked)
- Photons have no rest mass yet gravity bends their path. In general relativity, energy and momentum curve spacetime.
- Weightlessness on the ISS is free fall, not zero gravity: about 90% of surface gravity, roughly 15.5 orbits a day at about 7.66 km/s.
- For a uniform ball, gravity is zero at the centre. Earth is denser inside, so its gravity actually peaks near the core–mantle boundary before falling to zero.
- The SCI Cards facts for the women and Indian scientists checked so far: Janaki Ammal, Anna Mani, Kamala Sohonie, Bibha Chowdhuri, Asima Chatterjee, Dorothy Hodgkin, Lise Meitner, Marie Tharp, Henrietta Leavitt, Cecilia Payne-Gaposchkin.
