# Speed perspectives reel: voice-over to record (founder's own voice)

Record it in one go if you like. Leave a clear pause (about a second of quiet) between the numbered parts, so they can be cut apart. Speak at your natural pace.

1. How fast was the car? Galileo found this formula: speed is distance divided by time. That is its average speed.
2. When someone says a place is ten minutes away, they have intuitively assumed your average speed.
3. The Vaisheshika school of thought believed that vega is an inherent characteristic of a rolled object. Today we call it momentum.
4. The astronomer Bhaskara the Second figured out that the Earth revolves with a variable speed.
5. Two buses moving at the same speed. Each looks to be at rest for the other.
6. Myriam Hirt found a pattern. Speed increases with body size to a point, and then decreases.
7. J. C. Bose measured a plant's growth, and its movements.
8. Thinking happens in both. Waves in neurons travel at about a hundred metres each second, and ion flow in plants at a few centimetres each second.
9. Nothing in this universe with mass can cross the speed of light.
10. Except space. It is not travelling. It is stretching, and it has no speed limit.
11. Just one speed unit covers all these perspectives, and more.
