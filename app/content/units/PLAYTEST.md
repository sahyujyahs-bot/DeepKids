# Play-test: the checks on what a family prints, plays and does

The five checks in REWRITE_UNIT.md read each sentence on its own. These read the unit the way a family uses it: printed,
played, followed step by step, opened one card at a time. Every checker of a unit does both. Each rule comes from a
mistake the founder or a reviewer found in a live unit.

## A. Printables and games
1. **The printable is the real thing.** Every card, square, token and picture is written out one by one. "24 squares" means
   24 listed squares; "48 cards: 16 SUNLIGHT…" means 48 items. Never one line describing the printable.
2. **The count in the title matches the items.** "22 size cards" has 22 items.
3. **What the card promises, the card has.** If the rules say "the answer is on the back", every card is `{f, b}`. If they say
   "a picture", the card has a real picture (`img`), or the rule doesn't mention a picture.
4. **Nothing says "the app prints it" unless the app does.** A guide, gap template or half-picture the step needs is a printable
   item, or the step says how to draw it by hand.
5. **Play the whole game with 2 players, then with 4.** It has a clear end and a winner (or a shared goal). Nobody gets stuck
   with no move. Every card and every square does something. Every rule is needed. Piles that can run out say what happens.
6. **Players a family has.** A game works with 2 people (parent and child). More roles are optional extras.
7. **Ordering and sorting sheets don't print the answer.** Steps to put in order are not already in order.
8. **A "label the picture" item has the picture**, or asks the child to draw it first.

## B. Steps, checks and the rest of the unit agree
9. **Every check matches its step.** If a step says "don't correct it, it's a guess", the check doesn't mark one answer right.
10. **The unit never teaches what it calls a misconception.** Games, songs and acting follow the same science as the explanations.
11. **Numbers add up.** Step minutes add to the activity's minutes; a schedule's days match the steps ("stop watering for the last
    three days" means three days on the schedule); step ids have no gaps.
12. **A fair test is fair.** The thing kept the same really is kept the same.
13. **One person stays one person.** A made-up child keeps the same class, age and pronoun everywhere in the unit.
14. **Cards are in a sensible order.** A step that needs a skill comes after the step that teaches it.

## C. People and names
15. **Steps speak to the family.** No made-up name in anything the family does ("Fill a glass…", "Ask your child…",
    "Each player…"). Names belong in stories, and to real scientists.
16. **A name in a question is introduced at first mention** ("Kavya, a girl in your class, says…"), or replaced ("A friend says…").
17. **Pronouns match the person.** A boy is never "her"; an option never says "her" or "mine" when no such person is in the question.
18. **Vary the names.** Not the same four names across every unit. Mostly women and girls (WRITING.md rule 27), about 60% South Indian.
19. **No family is assumed.** Not every child has a grandmother at home or a sister. Say "a grandparent or an older relative, if you have one",
    or leave them out of the steps.
20. **Never "it" for a living being** (WRITING.md rule 26). One animal is "she" or "he"; "they" only for more than one, or when the sex is unknown and it reads naturally.

## D. Only family words reach families
21. **No internal notes in any field a family sees:** no "Title checked automatically", "Video id recalled", "Used in g1",
    "can replace the first three cards", "on the DeepKids list", "the founder's…", "still being written", file names or plan notes.
22. **Every link has a working address**, or it's a place with a location. A video is opened and checked before it goes in, or left out.

## E. Safety in proportion
23. **A safety line only for a real hazard:** flame, heat, spirit or other chemicals, chuna, sharp edges, glass, sun in the eyes,
    electricity from the mains, small parts with under-5s nearby. Never "Safety: None", never "stand on a dry floor" for looking
    in a mirror, never a doctor's check before one minute of star jumps.
24. **A side warning sits apart** (its own field), not inside the main step.

## F. Say it once, say it plainly
25. **Each idea once per unit.** The same exercise, question or fact doesn't come back in four places.
26. **No stock lines:** "The story also quietly asks…", "one source, showing up in many forms", and the shapes banned in WRITING.md rule 22.
27. **8 to 10 jokes, all real jokes** a child would laugh at.
28. **Name it where it's used.** Every card makes sense to someone who opens only that card: no "the sticky notes" or
    "how many elephants?" without saying which.
29. **No screen words on paper.** A printed game doesn't say "tap".

## G. It really works in a flat
30. **Do every core experiment in your head, step by step, in a city flat with the listed things**, and write the result you'd
    really get. If it's doubtful, say so, make it an optional extra, or replace it.
31. **Outdoor-only or fire steps are never in the core path.**
32. **Lead times are in the plan.** A plant that must grow for two weeks first appears in the schedule two weeks before.
33. **Story evidence isn't reused as fact** when the story itself says it's made up or unlikely.

## H. Proofread
34. Grammar and agreement ("they take in… and give out"), and every sentence says what it means.
