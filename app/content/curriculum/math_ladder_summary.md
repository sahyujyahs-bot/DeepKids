## Notes for merging

- New units have no board chapters. Existing ids are unchanged, so chapter links keep working.
- When the patch is merged, each existing unit just below a new rung needs that rung added to its `go_deep`, and each existing topic level needs the new unit ids added to its `subtopic_levels` (both can be worked out from the patch).
- Units with a note in `verify` carry a fact that must be checked again when the unit is written.
