# Books we still need

A list of the books DeepKids refers to that we do not have yet. Made on 27 October 2026 by reading every science unit, every class lesson, the board chapter files, the curriculum maps and the Library page.

"Free" means you can download it legally at no cost. "Buy" means it has to be bought (or borrowed from a library).

## The 10 most useful

1. **NCERT maths books, Classes 1 to 5** (5 books): *Joyful Mathematics* Class 1 and Class 2, *Maths Mela* Class 3, 4 and 5. **Free** at ncert.nic.in/textbook.php. The app's maths follows NCERT, but so far it only knows the chapter names (copied from other websites). With the books, maths lessons can follow the pages, the way science does.
2. **NCERT English books, Classes 1 to 5** (5 books): *Mridang* Class 1 and 2, *Santoor* Class 3, 4 and 5. **Free** at ncert.nic.in/textbook.php. English also follows NCERT in the app, with chapter names only.
3. **NCERT Hindi books, Classes 1 to 5** (5 books): *Sarangi* Class 1 and 2, *Veena* Class 3, 4 and 5. **Free** at ncert.nic.in/textbook.php. Same as English.
4. **Cambridge Primary curriculum frameworks**: Science (0097), Mathematics (0096) and English (0058). **Free** PDFs at cambridgeinternational.org (Programmes, Cambridge Primary, each subject's "curriculum framework"). The app tags lessons to these, but the wording was written by us and needs checking against the real document.
5. **Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 and Learner's Book 3, second edition** (Cambridge University Press, 2021, Jon Board and Alan Cross). **Buy.** We have the old 2014 first editions of Books 1 and 3, while Books 2, 4 and 5 are the second edition. Schools now use the second edition, so families on the Cambridge track will have different pages.
6. **Cambridge Primary Mathematics Learner's Books 1 to 5, second edition** (Cambridge University Press). **Buy.** The maths map points to Cambridge stages 110 times but has no book to follow page by page.
7. **Stuff Matters** by Mark Miodownik. **Buy.** Used in 9 places (5 materials units and 4 class lessons). The best grown-up source for the materials topic.
8. **The Heart of Understanding** by Thich Nhat Hanh. **Buy.** Quoted in the Roots cards of 8 units (the "interbeing" and "sheet of paper" ideas). Having it lets us check those quotes.
9. **Old religious and philosophy texts in the Roots cards**: the Upanishads (Chandogya is used 22 times), the Tao Te Ching (15 times), the Bhagavad Gita, Zhuangzi, the Buddhist suttas and others. **Free** in older translations (see section (c)). Having them lets us check every Roots quote.
10. **The Way Things Work Now** by David Macaulay. **Buy.** Used in 2 units, 2 class lessons and on the Library's "Worth buying" shelf.

## What we already have

- **Board textbooks already turned into lessons: 8 books, 89 chapters.** NCERT *Our Wondrous World* Class 3, 4 and 5 (32 chapters); Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Books 1 to 5 (30 units); NIOS Open Basic Education, Environmental Studies Level A and Level B (27 lessons). Their PDFs are not in the private books folder yet, so please keep a copy there.
- **Open textbooks** (free, mostly college level, used for checking facts): 44 listed in `knowledge/catalog.json` (OpenStax Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Astronomy and others), 32 of them downloaded in the private `books` folder.
- **Free PDFs** listed in `app/content/books/free_books.json`: 20 (for example *Concepts of Biology*, *Big Ideas in Astronomy*, *Bulli and the Tiger*, *Aesop's Fables*).
- The private `books_deepkids` folder is empty.
- **Children's books the lessons recommend: we have none of them.**
