# India-focused AI study, homeschool and tutoring apps for children (research, Oct 2026)

Scope: AI apps for children roughly aged 4–14 (Classes 1–8) aimed at India, with extra attention to small, recent (2023–2026) and homeschool-facing products. Compiled 30 Oct 2026 from web search.

**How this was gathered, and its limits**
- Only web search results were available. Fetching pages directly was blocked in this session, so most facts come from search-result snippets of company sites, app-store listings, press releases and news articles. Most are **self-reported by the company**. User counts, prices and funding should be treated as company claims.
- "(uncertain)" marks facts that sources disagree on, or that come from a single thin listing.
- Reddit (r/IndianParents, r/homeschool) and Instagram did not surface in the search index, so **no app here is confirmed as advertising on Instagram**. Apps that are tiny, new and consumer-facing (Karakbit, ZigZu, Nik Teacher, Kyloen, Arya, QikWorksheet, EaseLearn) are the likeliest to run Instagram ads, but that is a guess.
- Every app listed has at least one source URL. No app was added without a source.

## 7. Overlap with DeepKids Gurukul

DeepKids Gurukul: a planner and deep, topic-led lesson guide for Indian homeschooling parents of 6–10 year olds. It plans the year and each day, tracks progress against NCERT/Cambridge/NIOS, has hands-on home experiments, stories, big questions, printable worksheets, an AI "Guru", and school-transfer transcripts.

**Closest overlaps (most to least)**
1. **Nohkoo**: the only product found that combines an AI homeschool planner, a daily plan per child, an AI worksheet generator and progress tracking, with India as a country option. It is generic and global, with no topic-led depth, Indian stories or NIOS mapping found.
2. **Kuraplan**: AI lesson plans, printable worksheets for several children, and record-keeping for homeschoolers. Not India-specific.
3. **Asparents**: the only Indian app found that names homeschooling and targets Indian parents. It is guidance and support, not a planner or curriculum.
4. **Boostmychild**: Indian, NEP/NCF-aligned. AI weekly activities, worksheets and parent tips. Report cards and portfolios. It stops at age 8 and is school- and parent-oriented, not homeschool.
5. **QikWorksheet / AIWorksheetPro / TestGenie**: overlap only on printable NCERT/CBSE worksheets for Class 1–6 (Hindi, EVS, Maths, English). No planning or lessons.
6. **Kyloen / Nik Teacher / Educate AI / StudySathi / Braino / EaseLearn / Study100on100**: overlap with the AI "Guru" for Class 1–5 NCERT questions. These are child-facing doubt solvers, not parent-led lesson guides.
7. **Wippi / MyWonder / Arya / Gurukul Home**: overlap on stories, curiosity and Indian culture for ages 4–10, as companions or entertainment.
8. **Big platforms** (Byju's, PW, Vedantu, Cuemath): overlap only on AI doubt-solving and adaptive practice, mostly for older children.

**Name clash to watch:** "Gurukul" is crowded. GurukulGPT (AI tutor for kids on WhatsApp), Open Gurukul (free AI tutor), Gurukul Home (AI shloka companion), Gurukul AI (IIT Gandhinagar research platform, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28611)), GurukulOS (school software, [gurukulos.com](https://www.gurukulos.com/)) and Gurukula (culture app) all exist.

**Gaps no product found fills**
- **An Indian homeschool year and day planner.** Nothing found plans an Indian child's year and day against NCERT **and** NIOS **and** Cambridge Primary together.
- **School-transfer transcripts or portfolios for re-entry into Indian schools.** US planners do transcripts. No Indian product maps homeschool work to class-level NCERT outcomes for an admission or transfer.
- **Topic-led, deep lessons for the parent to teach.** Every Indian AI product found is chapter-led and child-facing: doubt solving, practice, tests. None guides a parent through a topic with roots, a deep truth and a two-session spine.
- **Hands-on experiments with household items, tied to the syllabus.** Experiment offerings are offline kits or classes (Flintobox, Curiosity Kids) or on-screen simulations for schools (Dencity). None is a planned home experiment inside a lesson.
- **"Big questions" and philosophy-style discussion for ages 6–10.** Not found. AugLi (critical thinking) is the nearest.
- **Indian stories woven into the curriculum.** Story apps (Arya, Katha Room, Vedapath) stand apart from schoolwork.
- **Coverage of Classes 1–5 in general.** Most AI tutors start at Class 6 (Edzy, Learning Panda, CBSETutor, EdSparks, padho.ai, IndiaSchool.ai, Eduro). The 6–10 age band is underserved apart from phonics apps and worksheet makers.
- **Waldorf, Montessori or Charlotte Mason-style guidance for Indian parents.** Nothing India-specific was found.
