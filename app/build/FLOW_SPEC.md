# The app flow, as the founder wants it (audit every step against the code)

## 1. Sign-up
- Google login, then parent's name and phone number.
- Phone: digits only, right length for India (10 digits, optional +91); later OTP verification. Check whether the DeepKids shop website already verifies or links phone numbers.
- Child's name and age. (Multiple children with one parent profile: later.)

## 2. Choose the track
- **DeepKids track** (topic-led): the universe of topics and subtopic units covering every board and more. Child is ready for any school. Curiosity allowed, but the plan nudges to stay level with the boards. One week each quarter: a sprint on topics trailing the boards. Paid.
- **Board track** (chapter-led): the chosen book(s) chapter by chapter, with our quizzes, big questions, challenging perspectives and curated extras. One or more boards: NCERT, NIOS, Cambridge. Free.
- Waldorf: decide whether it is a board or a separate approach.
- A separate paid tier for extra AI use (chat and other AI). Payment gateways later.
- The difference between the two tracks is explained on screen.

## 3. Subjects, levels, pace, days
- Choose subjects. For each subject choose a level, or let the child take a short placement quiz that sets the level.
- No level is locked: every level and subject can be browsed and queued. The year plan uses the chosen level.
- Pace per subject (changeable later). Week: which days are full, half or free.

## 4. Generate the plan
- DeepKids track: queue the subtopic units per subject in the right order, making sure everything the boards expect that year is covered by year end (and more).
- The queue changes as the child learns: a project or maths problem linked to another unit adds that unit to the queue for the next day. Parents can remove, reorder, or add from the library.
- Quarterly sprint week for trailing board topics.
- Daily plan from the minutes of each part. Multi-day experiments and projects run on their own schedules and each is tracked.
- Never clash with classes, tuition, outings, sleep or other fixed times.
- Board track: same, with chapters and their day-by-day lesson plans, quizzes, and DeepKids extras (worksheets, big questions, songs, jokes) pulled from the linked units.

## 5. Each day
- Parent prep: choose morning-of or night-before, with reminders.
- Warm-up: jokes and the song of the subtopic being studied (board chapters use their linked unit's song and jokes), then the daily game series (refresher questions from what has been learned).
- Lessons, plus the family's own plans and classes.

## 6. Status of every item
- Finished only when it is really done (things to fill or do are done). Items with nothing to fill can be marked finished at once.
- Started but not finished: Ongoing. Includes experiments and projects running over days.
- Lesson done but its quizzes, activities or projects not done: Pending.
- Pending vs Ongoing must be clear and not overlap.
- Only fully completed parts go to History and Progress: per unit, parts done out of total, and which ones.
