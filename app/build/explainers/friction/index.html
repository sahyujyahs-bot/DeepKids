Why doesn't your chappal slip?

It is pushing on a smooth stone step. So why does it stay put?
Look closer
Even a smooth floor is bumpy up close. The bumps catch on each other, like the teeth of two combs.
Closer still: atoms
Atoms on the two surfaces tug on each other. They hold on, then snap free as things slide. Each snap leaves them jiggling.
Jiggling atoms = heat
That is why rubbing makes things warm. Rub your hands and feel it!

Friction is the grip between two things that touch.
