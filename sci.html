SCI.

Scientist Trading Cards

Not just who discovered what. Each card takes one scientist, the fundamental truth their work uncovered, and shows you where that truth is hiding in everyday life — so science becomes something your child can spot, touch, and feel.

A new kind of collectible

Pokémon Has Cards. Why Not Scientists?

Kids collect Pokémon. They collect footballers, cricketers, superheroes. So why not the real people who cracked how the universe actually works? SCI. is a collectible deck of scientist trading cards — chase them, swap them, complete the set. Except every card is a real human who genuinely changed the world.

What makes a SCI. card different

Three Layers On Every Card

Layer 01
🔭

The Discovery

The breakthrough each scientist is remembered for — told like a story a kid actually remembers, not a date to memorise.

Layer 02
💡

The Fundamental Truth

The deeper law of nature underneath the discovery — the real why it matters, not a textbook definition.

Layer 03
🌍

Where You Live It

Where that truth shows up in ordinary life — so your child starts spotting the science in the world around them, not just on the card.

That's the whole idea: science you can identify and experience — not facts you file away and forget.

First from the deck

Meet The First Two

Common to legendary

Every Card Has A Rarity

See the glowing world behind each scientist — a distant planet, a blazing sun, a whole galaxy? That's not just art. The cosmic body behind the portrait marks how rare the card is. The brighter it burns, the harder it is to pull — and the more collectors will want it. Some scientists are common finds. A few are legendary.
